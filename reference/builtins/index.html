Builtin functions¶

The functions an expression may call, grouped by the kind of value they work on. This page is generated from rhema doc, so it lists exactly what the compiler accepts; calling one with the wrong number of arguments is an error the checker reports. A function given the wrong kind of value stops generation with a message naming both.

Numbers, text and rendering¶

Functions on numbers and text, and the two that write operators with the right brackets.

Function Arguments
str 1 The text of a number or other value.
paren 1 Its argument with brackets around it.
infix 3 infix($1, "+", $3): the two children joined by the operator text, bracketed as the precedence table requires.
prefix 2 prefix("\neg", $2): the operator text before the child, bracketed as the precedence table requires.
cond 3 cond(c, a, b): a when c is true, b otherwise.
abs 1 The absolute value of a number.
min 2 The smaller of two numbers.
max 2 The larger of two numbers.
bit_and 2 Bitwise and of two whole numbers; with bit_or, a way to track which of several items a subtree has used.
bit_or 2 Bitwise or of two whole numbers.
bit_xor 2 Bitwise exclusive or of two whole numbers.

Generators¶

Leaves that draw a whole number. They appear on the right of a production, or after random NAME =; their arguments are whole numbers.

Function Arguments
integer 1 integer(n): a whole number with exactly n digits.
nonzero_integer 1 nonzero_integer(n): as integer, never 0.
integer_between 2 integer_between(a, b): a whole number from a to b inclusive.
nonzero_integer_between 2 nonzero_integer_between(a, b): as integer_between, never 0.

A proposition answer is graded in the Boolean context.
